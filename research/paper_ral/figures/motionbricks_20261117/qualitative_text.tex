For Fig.~\ref{fig:qualitatives}, generated joint sequences are retargeted to
CARLA~0.9.16 pedestrian skeletons using analytical inverse kinematics (IK). A shared rigid
transform places pedestrian and vehicle paths in \texttt{Town10HD\_Opt}.
Selected frames from fixed vehicle-view and elevated cameras are
composited using semantic masks.

Fig.~\ref{fig:realization} illustrates a separate inference-time realization
with pretrained MotionBricks~\cite{wang2026motionbricks}. MotionBricks completes
the articulation of one 7.6\,s sample from sparse pose targets. Explicit retiming and root projection
either preserve the sampled trajectory or impose pose, timing and route edits.
Contact IK stabilizes planted feet after retargeting. The authored hold at 3--4\,s
yields 1.13\,s below 0.2\,m/s, and a 0.40\,m lateral edit increases the closest
vehicle-center separation from 2.18 to 2.58\,m. The vehicle path stays fixed.
We inspect all 30\,Hz frames. These authored scenarios are not new
\method predictions or validated human responses.
