- **II-D.** The FID critiques are one reference (Chong and Forsyth) instead of three. The closing sentence on

## What was added

- **Section V, per-condition metrics.** "The energy score [31], the samples' mean distance to the recording
  minus half their mean pairwise distance, is a proper score that rewards both (lower is better). We added
  it, a retrieval baseline and a second guidance scale after the test pass, fixing each before scoring it on
  test."
- **Section V, rule.** "three against a single-seed model" is "three against a single run", because the
  retrieval baseline is one run and not a model with a seed. The guidance scale is called $w$ where the
  selection is described.
- **Section VI-A, new paragraph after the regressor.** "A retrieval baseline returns the recorded root paths
  of the five training sequences whose vehicle path is nearest to the condition's. It beats the prior on mean
  ADE (2.77 versus 3.03 m) and ECS (0.16 versus 0.02) under our rule, so the vehicle path is informative
  without a trained model. EgoPed beats retrieval on both, while retrieval has the better minADE (1.25 m) and
  energy score (1.45 versus 1.77)."
- **Section VI-B, guidance paragraph rewritten.** "Guidance buys coupling only up to w=5. On validation,
  EgoPed's ECS is 0.25 without guidance (w=1) and 0.36 to 0.37 from 5 to 15. Over that range FID still falls
  from 4.61 to 3.90, while minADE rises from 1.22 to 1.43 m. At the FID-selected w=15 the prior has the better
  test energy score (1.51 versus 1.77) under our rule. At w=1, which has the best validation energy score of
  six scales, EgoPed's test score is 1.40, level with the prior and with retrieval. Its test ECS (0.18) and
  ADE (2.64 m) are then worse but still beat the prior under our rule, and its ECS no longer beats retrieval.
  We keep the pre-specified selection by FID, as is standard, but rank models by mean ADE and ECS."
- **Section VII, Metrics.** "At the reported guidance the prior has a better energy score than EgoPed
  (Section VI-B)."

## What the old text said that no longer holds

- "Guidance trades coverage for FID without changing coupling." True from 5 to 15 only. Without guidance the
  validation ECS is 0.25 instead of 0.36.
- "Selecting the scale by FID ... picks the least accurate setting." True within 5 to 15 only. Without
  guidance the mean ADE is worse than at 15.
- "... but rank models only by per-condition metrics." The energy score is a per-condition metric and puts
  the prior first at the reported guidance. The sentence now names mean ADE and ECS.

## What an independent read changed

A second reader checked the first version of this edit against the result files. Its findings are worked in.

- The test numbers at w=1 followed a sentence about validation without saying they are test numbers. On
  validation the same quantity is 1.17 and beats the prior, so the reading would have been wrong.
- The validation FID at w=1 (5.10) has no record in `research/data`. The paragraph no longer uses it.
- The paper said the test partition was evaluated once and then spoke of a first test pass. The sentence in
  Section V now discloses the three additions, in the way the Brier correction is disclosed.
- The first version gave only the endpoints 1 and 15, which hid that coupling is flat from 5 on. That is the
  part that bears on the reported scale and it also fits the heading of VI-B (FID keeps improving while
  coupling does not).
- EgoPed's lead over retrieval on ECS exists only with guidance. The paragraph now says so.
- The orientation of the energy score was not stated. The cited source defines it with the opposite sign.

Every number in the new sentences matches `research/data/energy_retrieval_scene_test.txt`,
`research/data/energy_retrieval_scene_val.txt`, `research/data/resplit_val_guidance_fid.txt` or the validation
table by guidance in `research/review_action_plan_2026-11-13.md`, and every "beats", "better" and "level"
matches the rule.

## Left as it is

- Section V still says "evaluated the test partition once, after all selection was fixed". Together with the
  new disclosure this reads as one planned pass with later additions named, as for the Brier correction.
- "Mean pairwise distance" names both Diversity and MM in feature space and the spread of the root paths in
  meters.
- Wording moves the page total in steps. Four words after "at inference" in Section III-D cost 81 pt, and one
  clause in the guidance paragraph decided between three free lines and a ninth page. Measure any further
  rewording with a build.
