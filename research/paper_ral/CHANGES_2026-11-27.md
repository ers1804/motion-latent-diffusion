## Changes on top of the Overleaf version

Sixteen lines differ from the file Erik sent.

- **The label is "Full sequence" with a capital, as "Full-seq" was** (line 34, the macro). In lowercase the
  table rows and one sentence started with a small letter, and "0.632 m for full sequence" after "over the
  first 1.95 s" read as "over the full sequence". As a name it now stands like "EgoPed".
- **Hyphen before a noun** (lines 146, 172, 261, 350). "The full-sequence ablation", "a full-sequence
  variant" and twice "trainable full-sequence run" are written out, as "full-sequence conditioning" and
  "full-sequence attention" already were.
- **Table II was 11 pt too wide** (line 284). The longer label pushed the table into the margin. The row
  "Full sequence, encoder trainable (1 seed)" is "Full sequence, trainable (1 seed)", which is how the text
  names that run.
- **Minus signs in the intervals of Table III** (lines 305 to 312). The 14 negative bounds were set with a
  hyphen ("[-0.18, -0.05]") next to a true minus in the value. This is older than the revision.
- **"We build" in the third contribution** (line 114, was "We provide"). The conclusion now releases the
  nuScenes and staged part only, which is 2,226 of the 5,668 recorded sequences, so the contribution must not
  read as a release of all of them. Section IV-A already says "we built one".
- **Fig. 3.** The legend says "Full sequence" and "Full sequence, trainable"
  (`research/paper/figures/make_fid_vs_conditioning.py`). The label with "encoder" made the figure 20 pt
  wider, which would have shrunk the plot. The script also embeds TrueType fonts now. Fig. 3 was the only
  figure with Type 3 fonts, which some IEEE PDF checks reject. Before the change the script reproduced the
  figure of the paper pixel for pixel, and the new one has the same size and the same points.

The Table III caption has three lines in place of two. A shorter opening ("Test contrasts are ...") still
gives three, so the wording of Overleaf is kept.

Three more changes were agreed with Erik after the first report.

- **The conclusion names the Waymo part as withheld** (line 447). "Code, trained models and the nuScenes and
  staged parts of the paired dataset will be released with this paper, but not the Waymo part or the everyday
  drives." Before, only the everyday drives were named, and a reader could not tell that the Waymo sequences
  (3,442 of 5,668) are not released.
- **"differences of unrounded seed means" in the Table III caption** (line 296). Six cells differ by 0.01 from
  the difference of Table II's rounded means, for example +0.59 against 3.03 and 2.43.
- **Table I caption says what the counts leave out** (line 212). "Counts include mirrored copies (5,668
  recorded sequences, each mirrored once) and leave out tracks under 2 s, one of them staged." Section IV-A
  speaks of 17 staged scenarios and Table I holds 16 recorded staged sequences. The seventeenth (scene 0017,
  21 frames, in the training partition) is shorter than the 40 frames the loader needs.
