## What changed and why

- **The regressor in the first contribution and the conclusion**, as in the abstract (entries 2 and 14). Both
  now end "against 1.97 m and 0.08 for a deterministic regressor". They give the error as 3.03 to 2.43 m in
  place of a gain of 0.59 m, so that the regressor's 1.97 m can stand next to it.
- **Full sentences.** The three contributions (entries 2 to 4) and the opening phrase of eight captions
  (5 to 12) had no verb. One limitation began with "It" and took its subject from the heading (13). Run-in
  headings in bold or italics, such as "Body pose quality." or "(a) Ego-encoder pretraining.", are headings
  and stay as they are.
- **American spelling.** The 21 words in the table at the end, plus "colour" in entry 9 and "favours" in
  entry 15. Two of them are vocabulary. "pavements" is now "sidewalks", because an American reader takes
  pavement for the road surface, and "kerb" is "curb". Titles in the reference list stay as published.
- **Space.** Entry 1 and the two cuts in entry 15 pay for the regressor clauses. Entry 1 must go in together
  with entry 2. Without it the first contribution gains a line, the last equation of Section III no longer
  fits on page 2, and the paper runs to 9 pages.
