  prior matches or is level with the best conditional model. They now say it ranks first, as decided on
  2026-11-15. Entries 2, 4 and 44.

  mean ADE of all models (1.97 m) and almost no coupling (0.08), and the abstract led with EgoPed's gain over
  the prior without saying so. Its lead sentence now ends "against 1.97 m and 0.08 for a deterministic
  regressor" (entry 1). Five words pay for it. "existing", "directly" and "trajectory" before "encoder" go
  in entry 1, and "the two design choices it favours both raise" becomes "both design choices it favours
  raise" in entry 2. The abstract has 1,197 of 1,200 characters.
- **Retraining with the corrected ego frame** improved none of the seven conditional models on mean ADE or ECS
  on validation, so the reported models stay. One new sentence in Section IV-A (entry 5), and the clause on
