### How the layout was made

Nothing is placed by hand. `research/src/teaser_map.py` finds where the teaser window lies in the recorded
scene by a rigid fit of the recorded vehicle path and the pedestrian's first second (0.08 m and 0.10 m rms,
the mirrored placement fits 1.9 m and 0.6 m, any other window 0.8 m and 1.2 m) and brings the map into the
frame of the figure. It refuses a placement that is not unique, a scene of the wrong length, a vehicle path
that leaves the mapped road, and a road surface that leaks through a gap in its edges. Schematic are the
zebra bars inside a mapped crosswalk, the rhythm of dashed lines, the nuScenes road dividers (the map gives
them no type) and the vehicle body.

    N=research/data/teaser_rs1_pooled_scene_val.npz
    <shapely 2.1 env>/python research/src/teaser_map.py build --npz $N
    M=research/data/teaser_map_rs1_pooled_scene_val.json      # then, in research/paper/figures:
    blender -b -P render_teaser.py -- --npz $N --row 39 --out hi/t39.png --res 2200 880 --samples 96 \
        --cam -0.75 22 16 --target -0.75 -3.5 0 --lens 25 --map $M --after
    blender -b -P render_teaser.py -- --npz $N --row 115 --out hi/t115.png --res 2200 880 --samples 96 \
        --cam -18 22 13.5 --target -4.4 -3.5 0 --lens 31.5 --map $M --car-times 0 4.8 9.75
    python make_teaser.py --npz $N --renders hi --rows 39 115 --out teaser.pdf

The environment used for the build was `nuscenes`. A Waymo row needs the stage `waymo-raw` first (environment
`drivestudio_waymo`). The samples of both rows come from `research/src/teaser_generate.py` with the seed-1234
checkpoint at epoch 1399, guidance 15 and 8 noise seeds, and row 39 is bit-identical to the earlier file.
Tests are in `research/src/test_teaser_map.py`.

### Two findings about the stored geometry

Neither changes a number in the paper. Both came up while the scenes were placed on the map.

- **The stored pedestrian path is about 5% longer than the recorded one.** For row 115 the root path in the
  motion representation has a net displacement of 15.30 m, the box track of the same pedestrian in the same
  window 14.51 m. `uniform_skeleton` (`mld/data/humanml/scripts/motion_process.py`, lines 21 to 27) scales
  the root positions by the ratio of the template's leg length to the source skeleton's. The stored path is
  also turned by 2 to 4 degrees against the box track. The vehicle path is metric (it fits the recorded
  poses to 0.02 m). ADE, FDE and the coupling score compare stored with generated motion in the same space,
  so they are consistent. Distances between pedestrian and vehicle are off by up to about 1 m after 15 m of
  walking, which is why the fit above uses only the pedestrian's first second.
- **The nuScenes trajectory is that of the roof lidar.** The processed nuScenes scenes store the lidar pose
  (1.84 m above the ground, 0.94 m ahead of the rear axle) as the vehicle pose. The Waymo scenes store the
  Waymo vehicle frame, whose origin lies 1.5 m behind the front camera, which puts it near the rear axle.
  "Ego position" therefore differs by about 1 m along the vehicle between the two sources. The figure draws
  the vehicle body around the right point for each.
