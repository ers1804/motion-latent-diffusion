## What his version changed in Sections VI-C to VIII

To pay for the second figure and its paragraph (about 40 lines), his version shortens every paragraph from
Section VI-C to the conclusion and both table captions. The numbers all survived. I kept the shortening and
changed 25 sentences (entries below), for these reasons.

- **Statements that had become wrong or contradictory.**
  - The 60 pedestrians near the path. "Neither ADE nor ECS (0.18 versus 0.03) beats the prior" had lost
    "under our rule" and contradicted its own numbers.
  - "114 held-out everyday driving scenes". "Held-out" means the validation and test partitions elsewhere.
    It is "scenes of everyday drives of our research vehicle" again, which the conclusion needs.
  - The braking shift of -0.29 holds among the fast vehicles where the recorded pedestrian stops (187
    conditions). The short form read as if it held for all recorded stops.
  - "The weakest coupling among the four conditional models". Table II has five conditional models, and
    the fifth (trainable Full-seq, ECS 0.04) couples less. It is "four main models" again.
  - "All differences to stationary, foreign or zeroed input meet our rule". True for ADE and ECS
    (`research/data/resplit_ego_controls.txt`), not for the Brier score of the zeroed input. It now says
    "all ADE and ECS gaps".
  - "The 0.07 m ADE gap" had lost what it is a gap to (the frozen encoder).
  - "Its 0.60 m ADE gain" beside 0.59 m in Section VI-A. The 0.60 m is the value of the 99% subset, so it
    is "a 0.60 m ADE gain".
- **One statement of ours that the read showed to be too strong.** "A single-seed trainable Full-seq run
  is nearly ego-blind." Its ADE (2.67 m) is far better than the prior's (3.03 m), so it is blind on ECS
  only. It now "barely couples".
- **Leftovers and unclear wording.** "Horizon rows extend $S$ seconds" in the caption of Table V (no row is
  called horizon and $S$ is not defined), "a foreign vehicle", "57% of recorded stops stand", "improves
  prior ADE", "the prior improves by 0.06 m" (over what), "beyond 2.5 m of", "(2.60 m)" given as a gain,
  "its weakest coupling", "inaccurate designs", "data" as a plural, and "scene-disjoint" for the split the
  paper calls scene-level.
- **Three clauses of reasoning restored.** Pretraining helps only while the encoder stays frozen. The model
  reacts confidently to the wrong vehicle. The stops that stand are "pedestrians who stand throughout,
  within a 1 m radius". They cost two lines. They are paid for by one sentence in the first qualitative
  paragraph that repeated the caption of Fig. 4 ("We render the medoid of eight root paths ...") and by a
  shorter sentence on robots in the limitations.
- **Release.** The last sentence is "but not the everyday drives" again, as agreed on 2026-11-16.

Still lost from the version of 2026-11-16, for lack of space: that walkers mostly face along the road (the
reason for the angle cue), the sentence "Had we ranked by FID, this paper would recommend ...", "FID cannot
certify such a model", and the pointer from the limitations to the braking edit.
