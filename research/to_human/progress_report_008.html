Key Insights

  • 🎯
    Cross-attention eliminates over-conditioning. H2's pooled single ego token (2 K/V tokens) becomes over-constraining at high CFG. H4's 196 K/V tokens (full T=196 ego sequence) provide distributed conditioning that doesn't collapse — the model can selectively attend to relevant trajectory parts.
  • 📉
    R-prec is a training-epoch effect, not a CFG effect. H4 R-prec@1 = 0.510–0.540 across all CFG levels (flat). This is mid-training behavior (epoch 3199 = 64% of H2's best epoch 4399). R-prec is improving: 0.510 → 0.529 → higher as training progresses. Expected to match H2 level (0.671) by segment-2 completion.
  • 🔀
    H4 MultiModality = 4.117 (H2 = 3.503 at CFG=5). H4 generates more diverse samples per conditioning — cross-attention enables richer conditioning without collapsing the generative diversity. This is architecturally desirable.
  • ⚡
    H4 converges faster on FID. At only 64% of H2's training, H4 is already 40–51% better on FID. This suggests the architecture change is genuinely better, not just faster to converge to the same place.

Why H4 FID is Flat: Mechanistic Hypothesis

H2 over-conditioning: When CFG guidance scale increases, the model is pushed strongly toward the conditioning signal. With H2's pooled ego token, this conditioning signal is informationally compressed (2 tokens → 256D vector). High CFG forces the model into regions of latent space that are densely conditioned by this compressed signal → mode collapse on the ego trajectory → higher FID.

H4 graceful scaling: H4's 196 K/V ego tokens are informationally rich. Each diffusion step can attend to the full temporal structure of the ego trajectory. Higher CFG strengthens conditioning without causing collapse, because the conditioning space is high-dimensional and well-structured. The model has more "room" to be conditioned without overfitting to a single compressed vector.
