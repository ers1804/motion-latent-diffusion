Key Research Insights

Literature Context

  • MLD (CVPR 2023): Core architecture. Latent diffusion in VAE space outperforms raw-space diffusion by 50×. We inherit: HumanML3D 263D, VAE latent 256D, DDIM sampling.
  • UniTraj (ECCV 2024): Multi-dataset (Waymo + nuScenes + Argoverse) with ego trajectory conditioning — same conditioning modality and datasets as ours. Validates the approach.
  • LED (CVPR 2023): Latent diffusion for trajectory prediction on nuScenes. Finding: latent-space diffusion >> raw-space. Supports H3 (larger latent should help).
  • RIG / InterGen: Reactive/interactive 3D body motion using cross-attention. Our ego-conditioned setup is a special case of RIG where the "other agent" is a vehicle trajectory.
  • WoSAD (2024): Very close to our approach — ego odometry encoded as polyline, cross-attended in diffusion denoiser. Key difference: they do 2D trajectory, we do 3D body motion.
