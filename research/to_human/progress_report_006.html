The Key Insight Driving H4

H3 gave us an unexpected diagnostic result: increasing latent dimension from 4 to 8 made FID 14.5% worse, but R-precision was essentially unchanged (0.671 → 0.676). This decoupling reveals that R-precision is entirely limited by the ego encoder architecture, not the latent space.

The current EgoEncoderPooled compresses 196 ego timesteps to a single mean-pooled 256d token before injection into the denoiser. H4 changes this fundamentally:

H2/H3 (trans_enc): Ego → transformer → mean-pool → (B, 1, 256) → concat with z → self-attn H4 (trans_dec): Ego → transformer → (B, 196, 256) → cross-attn K/V; z is Query

In H4, every denoising step the latent z can query "what was happening at ego timestep t=73?" or "how did the vehicle accelerate in the last 20 frames?" — all temporal detail is preserved.
