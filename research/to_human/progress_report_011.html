🔬 Why H6 Failed: MultiModality Collapse
MultiModality (higher = more diverse per condition)
Mechanism: When FREEZE_EGO=False, the ego encoder co-adapts with the denoiser. It learns highly discriminative per-condition features — each ego trajectory is encoded into a narrow, low-entropy representation. The denoiser then generates near-deterministic outputs per condition (MM collapse: 2.5 vs H4 4.0).
Literature alignment: Stable Diffusion, DALL-E 2, DreamBooth — all use frozen CLIP/DINO encoders. Unfreezing alongside the UNet/denoiser is known to cause generation collapse. IP-Adapter and ControlNet are the established patterns for adding conditioning without destroying diversity — both keep the backbone frozen.
Conclusion: FREEZE_EGO=True (H4) is the correct design. The encoder was pretrained for contrastive alignment — this pretraining gives it the right inductive bias. Unfreezing destroys the alignment and collapses diversity.
📝 Paper Narrative — Three-Part Story
01

Architecture: Cross-Attention Ego Conditioning

Conditioning on the full 196-step ego trajectory via cross-attention (H4, trans_dec) vs a single mean-pooled token (H2, trans_enc). The denoiser can attend to specific moments in the ego trajectory, producing temporally-aligned motion.

Main result: FID 6.603 → 3.392 (−49%), non-overlapping CIs.
02

Ablation: Encoder Freeze is Critical

H6 tests FREEZE_EGO=False — allowing the ego encoder to co-adapt with the denoiser. Results: MultiModality collapses (3.956→2.618), FID worsens (3.392→5.079), R@1 worsens (0.548→0.352). Full unfreezing is definitively the wrong approach.

Finding: Frozen encoder is necessary for generative diversity.
03

Design Recommendation

Frozen ego encoder + cross-attention denoiser is the correct architecture for ego-conditioned motion generation. This is consistent with the broader literature: frozen conditioning encoders (CLIP, DINO) preserve generative diversity while providing rich conditioning signals.

Future work: IP-Adapter style adapters on frozen H4 could improve R@1 without MM collapse.
