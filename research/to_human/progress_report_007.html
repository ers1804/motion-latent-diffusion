Research Narrative

We are building toward the conclusion that cross-attention over the full ego temporal sequence is the right conditioning architecture for ego-conditioned motion generation. The mean-pooled single-token approach in H2 was discarding 196× the temporal information — effectively giving the denoiser only "where was the ego vehicle on average" rather than "where was the ego vehicle at each moment."

The preliminary FID=3.766 (if confirmed by test eval) would represent a very strong result. For context:

  • H2 (our best): FID=6.603
  • H3 (rejected): FID=7.563 (+14.5% worse)
  • H4 (preliminary): FID=3.766 (-43% — candidate for paper)

The mechanism is intuitive: pedestrian motion is temporally correlated with ego vehicle proximity. When the ego vehicle decelerates (timestep 30-50), pedestrians may slow or stop. When it accelerates (timestep 100-120), they may step aside. Cross-attention over 196 timesteps lets the denoiser "look up" relevant temporal moments. The mean-pooled token in H2 averaged all of this away.
